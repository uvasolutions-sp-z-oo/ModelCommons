# ModelCommons Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to a respectful, inclusive, technically rigorous community where
people can participate without harassment, intimidation, or discrimination.
This applies to maintainers, contributors, users, researchers, and anyone
representing the project in public or private project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- State uncertainty and distinguish implemented, tested, experimental, and
  proposed behavior.
- Respect privacy, consent, licenses, attribution, and security boundaries.
- Use synthetic data in issues and examples.
- Welcome different experience levels and make room for correction.
- De-escalate conflict and accept reasonable moderator direction.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, discriminatory language, or unwanted sexual
  attention.
- Personal attacks, deliberate humiliation, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Pressuring anyone to reveal medical, employment, identity, or security data.
- Sharing credentials, private prompts, customer records, medical data, signing
  material, or uncoordinated vulnerability details.
- Misrepresenting local models as proprietary provider models or overstating
  safety, clinical validity, benchmark evidence, or device verification.
- Any conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a
  professional open-source community.

## Scope

This code applies in repositories, issue trackers, reviews, discussions, events,
project-affiliated chats, and public spaces when someone represents ModelCommons
or its community.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. If a report
contains a security issue, use the GitHub Security Advisory workflow described
in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not retaliate against a person who raises a
good-faith concern.

Maintainers will protect reporter privacy as far as reasonably possible, gather
context, avoid conflicts of interest, and respond proportionately. Responses may
include a private correction, warning, temporary restriction, removal from
project spaces, or permanent exclusion. Illegal threats may be escalated to the
appropriate service operator or authority.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code consistently may be removed
from enforcement responsibilities by other project maintainers.
